# Chapter 1 --- Product Definition

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Frozen after approval

## 1.1 Purpose

Sentinel AI is an enterprise-grade AI Digital Media Forensics Platform
for explainable verification of audio, video and image authenticity.

## 1.2 Repository Information

  Property       Value
  -------------- -------------------------------------
  Repository     ai-digital-media-forensics-platform

  Type           Monorepo

## 1.3 Vision

Sentinel AI aims to make digital media verification accessible,
transparent, and trustworthy. Our vision is to provide an AI-powered
forensics platform that helps individuals and organizations determine
the authenticity of audio, video, and image content. Every analysis
should be explainable, reproducible, and backed by clear evidence rather
than opaque predictions. The platform is designed to evolve with
emerging synthetic media technologies while maintaining engineering
excellence. We aspire to set a benchmark for trustworthy AI-powered
digital media forensics.

## 1.4 Mission

Deliver accurate, scalable and explainable digital media forensics
through production-grade AI and software engineering.

## 1.5 Problem Statement

Synthetic media is increasingly used for fraud and misinformation while
trustworthy verification tools remain limited. Sentinel AI provides
unified analysis with explainable AI, confidence scoring and
evidence-oriented reports.

## 1.6 Target Users

Primary: Individuals, Cybersecurity Teams, SOCs, Banks, Investigators,
Law Enforcement.

Secondary: Journalists, Researchers, Universities, Enterprises,
Governments and Moderation Teams.

## 1.7 Product Scope

MVP includes audio, video and image forensics, deepfake detection,
explainability, REST APIs and web dashboard. Future scope includes
mobile apps, browser extension, enterprise deployment and SDKs.

## 1.8 Product Positioning

An engineering-first AI Digital Media Forensics Platform.

## 1.9 Non-Goals

Not a media editor, social platform, cloud storage, streaming platform,
AI assistant, training platform or replacement for forensic experts.

## 1.10 Core Principles

Privacy First, Explainability, Security by Design, Engineering
Excellence, Transparency, Scalability, Reproducibility, Maintainability,
Extensibility and Responsible AI.

## 1.11 Success Criteria

Qualitative metrics now; measurable engineering metrics before v1.0.0.

## 1.12 Summary

Defines the identity, vision and scope of Sentinel AI.
